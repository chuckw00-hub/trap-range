# Trap Range

A first-person clay target trap shooting game set in a rural Northeast US field. Single-file, runs in any modern browser.

**▶ Play: https://chuckw00-hub.github.io/trap-range/**

## Controls

- **Aim** — mouse or trackpad moves your sight with reduced sensitivity and smooth tracking (cursor is captured while shooting; press **Esc** to free it)
- **Press & hold** (or **Spacebar**) — calls the pull and launches the clay
- **Release** — fires a shot; lead your target. Press and release again for shots two and three (mouse, touch, or Spacebar).

10 pulls per round, 3 shells each, with two clays on every pull. An animated bird makes a 5.8-second pass at about 28–40 metres on three randomly chosen pulls per round for +5 bonus points; it takes the same pattern spread as a clay. After breaking both clays, use your third shot on the bird; the pull stays open while it is in range. Two lighting scenarios (October foliage and high summer) are switchable from the start screen.

On phones and tablets, drag to track the clay and release to fire.

## Tech

Built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) (loaded from CDN). No build step — just open `index.html`.
